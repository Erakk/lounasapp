# Lunch places in central Tampere: research notes for the lounas app

*Compiled 21 Nov 2026 (gap-filling pass done the same day). Origin point: **Kehräsaari** (OSM node 1835267025, lat 61.49557, lon 23.76366), between Laukontori and Koskikeskus.*

---

## 1. Scope and method

**Goal.** Find as many lunch places as possible in and around central Tampere. For each one, record what the app needs:

| App field | What was collected |
|---|---|
| Distance | Straight-line metres from Kehräsaari (geocoded with OpenStreetMap Nominatim). Walking time = air distance × 1.3 detour factor ÷ 80 m/min. **These are estimates only.** |
| Food type | Cuisine label + lunch format (buffet / plated / salad table + main / soup / pay-by-weight) |
| Price | Adult weekday lunch price in €, as listed. Student, pensioner, child, soup and salad variants noted where known. |
| Group size | **Estimated** comfortable group size, based on format, venue type and any stated seat counts or reservation rules. Seat counts and booking rules are quoted where the restaurant publishes them (see §4). |
| Dislike risk | **Estimated** chance that someone in a mixed group won't want to go: spicy-only, raw fish, vegan-only, single-dish menus, high price, theme venues and so on (see §5). |

**Sources (week 47–48/2026 unless noted):**
- **lounaat.info/tampere**: ~104 listings; ~65 in or near the centre. Gives address, website, lunch hours, rating, cuisine tag and this week's menus with prices.
- **lounasmenu.fi/tampere**: extra places plus per-place lunch pages (Pons, Tivoli, Los Pollos, Frenckell & Piha, Zeytuun, Astor, Faasai, Thai & Laos, Itsudemo, Nittaya).
- **The restaurants' own websites**, checked 21.11.2026: Koski, Salud, Harald, Suoma, Plevna, Myllärit, Piha, Amurin Helmi, Tammerin Kellari (Raflaamo), Valssi (Vapriikki), Fiorina, Zafran, Coriander, Talvipuutarha, Ståhlberg (3 locations), Boulangerie Marco, Gopal, Daiwo Sushi, Nanda Devi, Aloha Ramen, Osaka Teppanyaki, Sodexo, Juvenes, Compass Group.
- **Time Out "Tampereen parhaat lounaat"** (16 Mar 2026), **toptravels.fi**, **onnileo.fi**.
- Visit Tampere / Visit Finland / HappyCow / Wanderlog / Koskikeskus listings for addresses and reviews.
- **Shopping centre and market hall directories** (koskikeskus.fi, ratina.fi with weekly lunch lists, kauppahalli.tampere.fi), plus burger chain sites (Friends & Brgrs, Boneless, Hamptown, Burgers & Wine, Burger Company), for §2.6–2.7.

**Caveats.**
- ✔ = price confirmed this week from the restaurant's own site or this week's menu. Other prices come from aggregators and may be older.
- "Not published" means I looked on the restaurant's site and the aggregators and found no lunch price.
- Group size and dislike risk are **my estimates** unless a seat count or booking rule is quoted.

Heuristic for everyone else:

| Venue type | Default comfortable group | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Large staff / student canteen (Aleksis, Å11, Linna, Minerva, Juvenes Saarni/Dynamo, Myllärit, Finlayson Meetings) | 2–20 | Many seats, several lines, fast turnover |

| Known to be packed at peak (Biáng, Luckiefun's, Sayam9, Marco) | 1–4, or go before 11:15 / after 13:00 | |

## 5. Dislike risk ("would someone object?")

The risk is per place, but the real test is **group compatibility**: one person with a strong dislike can veto a place. Proposed scoring:

| Factor | Example places | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Broad buffet with meat, fish **and** a veg/vegan dish | Ståhlberg, Puisto, Eflatun, Semafori, Onni & Leo, Aleksis, Valssi, Suoma, Myllärit | **Low** |
| Finnish home cooking, plated, 2–3 choices | Mummola, Kotilounas, Tampella, Pons | Low |
| Single-cuisine with spice (Thai, Indian, Malaysian, Sichuan-style) | Sayam9, Faasai, Thai & Laos, Nittaya, Borneo, Biáng, Nanda Devi, Delhi House, Coriander | Medium (spice intolerance) |
| Raw fish focus | Itsudemo, Daiwo, Luckiefun's, Osaka | Medium–High (pregnancy, dislike of raw fish) |
| Single-dish concept | Burgers (Social, Zarillo), ramen, chicken (Los Pollos), open sandwiches (Tivoli), soup-only (Moro, Amurin Helmi), schnitzel | Medium |
| **Vegan-only** | Gopal, Papu ja Pata | High for committed meat-eaters, **lowest** for vegans |
| Price ≥ 18 € | Astor, Henriks, 4 Vuodenaikaa, Harald premium, Salud grill | Medium–High (budget) |
| Pub / theme atmosphere | Harald, Plevna | Low–Medium |
| Low ratings | Piha (2.2), Food & Co Reaktori (2.0) | Medium |

**Dietary flags available from menus.** Finnish menus use L (lactose-free), M (dairy-free), G (gluten-free), VE/VEG/V (vegan), VL (low-lactose), K (vegetarian), A (contains allergens), VS (fresh garlic), Mu (egg-free). The app can parse these to show "has vegan / GF option today".

**Allergen caveats found.** Robert's Coffee does not guarantee gluten-free food. Funky Woo dishes contain fish sauce and nuts. Astor is mostly GF and lactose-free.

## 8. Remaining gaps

1. **Prices still not published anywhere:** Sayam9, Osaka Teppanyaki (only in an image), Luckiefun's, Aloha Ramen, Lam (lunch), Pizzeria Napoli (lunch), Coriander, Talvipuutarha, Compass staff restaurants (Å11, Naulankanta, Valtatie 30, Minerva guest price). The Compass ones can be read from the feed on a weekday. The rest need a phone call or a visit.
2. **Status to confirm:** Bistro Papu ja Pata (website gone), Amurin Helmi (museum closed until 2027), Taj, Funky Woo's weekday hours, Ståhlberg Tulli on Fridays, Sodexo Linna's price columns.
3. **Address conflicts:** Salud (Tuomiokirkonkatu 19 vs Aleksanterinkatu 32; its own site says the latter), Los Pollos (Aleksanterinkatu 20 vs Koskikeskus).
4. **Group size** is still a heuristic outside the 10 venues in §4. Add a "how did it go with N people?" feedback loop in the app.
5. **Walking times** are air distance × 1.3. Use a routing API (OSRM / GraphHopper foot profile) for real times.
6. Koskikeskus, Ratina and Kauppahalli are now catalogued (§2.6). Tullintori and the Sokos/Stockmann-area cafés are not.
7. **Missing prices in the clusters:** Ohana, Natsu, Pyörykkäbaari, Ranger, Café de Halle, Umami, Seoul Good, Rax, Moi Poke, Super Kebab, Taco Bell. Fast-food chains publish menu prices in their apps, not as lunch offers.
8. **Completeness lesson:** lunch aggregators miss most ethnic and small places, and OpenStreetMap misses new openings (Umaya, OBANH, Noodle Master, Toor Shahzada, the second Itsudemo). The most complete source was **Google Maps category searches**. For the app, consider seeding places from the Google Places API (paid; check its terms) or OSM, plus a "suggest a place" button.
9. **App modelling:** treat Koskikeskus, Kauppahalli and Ratina as **clusters** (one location with many options). That suits split-preference groups, and the cluster's dislike risk is effectively low.

## 9. Whole city (25.11.2026)

The app now lets you choose any starting point inside the City of Tampere, so the survey was extended from the centre to the whole city. The data lives in `data/places.city.ts`, separate from the hand-checked central list.

**Result:** 196 places added (362 in total, up from 168). 241 lie within 1.5 km of Kehräsaari; 121 are further out (Hervanta, Kaleva/Kauppi, Lielahti, Tesoma, Nekala/Viinikka, Hatanpää, Kaukajärvi, Vuores, Rusko, Aitolahti…). 61 are marked *verify* (in the app: "Tarkista").

**Method (same criteria as §1):**
1. **OpenStreetMap:** every `restaurant`, `fast_food`, `cafe` and `food_court` inside the city boundary (598). The 231 that were over 1.3 km from Kehräsaari and not yet in the data became candidates.
2. **lounaat.info:** the Tampere listing (105) plus every place page reachable through the "Lounasravintoloita lähistöllä" links (311 pages, 250 in Tampere). This surfaced places the listing never shows, including about 60 central ones the first survey missed (e.g. Rookie's, Urbano, Sorella, Smokehouse Bros, Tuhto, Tbilisi, PSTA).
3. **Own websites** of both groups, scanned for "lounas" lines, lunch hours and € prices.
4. **Campus restaurants** from the universities' student guide and Juvenes' own pages: non-students pay €9.05–9.55.
5. **Coordinates:** taken from the matching OpenStreetMap entry or by address lookup (Nominatim). Wrong-branch matches for chains were corrected by hand. Every place is checked against the city boundary by `npm run validate`.

**Rules:**
- **Confirmed lunch (lounaat.info hours or menu, or the restaurant's own page):** status *open*. The price counts as verified only when it came from this week's menu or the restaurant's own page.
- **Pizza/kebab places and senior service-centre restaurants that are open over lunch per OpenStreetMap,** with no lunch offer confirmed: status *verify*. This is the same rule as §2.8b.
- **Categories, format, group size and dislike risk:** drafted from the place's name, lounaat.info tags and menu using the same rules as §4–5, then reviewed one by one.
- **New cluster:** Duo in Hervanta, with Bai Wei, Caffitella, FireWok, Gate of India, Koto and Zarillo.

**Corrections to the central list:**
- **Papu ja Pata:** moved from Laukontori to Sammonkatu 15, where it is a vegan lunch buffet. The old entry was removed.
- **Baglung Durbar:** its site said it was closed, and KYRA (an Indian buffet) now runs at the same address, Aleksanterinkatu 22 C. Replaced.
- **Clava:** listed at Kirkkokatu 10, Koski's address, and also advertises a business lunch. It is probably Koski renamed, so it was not added. Check this.

**Left out:**
- Pages with no menu or hours.
- Evening-only places.
- Places outside the city (Pirkkala, Ylöjärvi, Lempäälä/Ideapark).
- Duplicates of existing places.
- Staff-only canteens.
- Hesburger, McDonald's, Subway and Kotipizza outside the shopping-centre clusters, as these chains have no lunch offer of their own.

**Gaps:**
- **No price:** 190 of the 362 places have none: 137 of the new ones and 53 of the central ones. Most lounaat.info pages say "katso lounaslista ravintolan sivuilta" and the restaurant's own page shows only the daily menu.
- **Estimated lunch hours:** where only opening hours were known, the place's info says "Lounasaika arvio".
