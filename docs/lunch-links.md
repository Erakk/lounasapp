- Where a restaurant has its own lunch page, prefer it over aggregators (lounaat.info, lounasmenu.fi). Several aggregator links can be swapped for own pages during app data building (M0).
- Ratina's store pages (`ratina.fi/liikkeet/<shop>/#lunch`) hold weekly lunch lists for every Ratina restaurant. That is the most reliable source for that cluster.
- Script-rendered pages (Osaka, Luckiefun's, Whatever, Seoul Good, Itsuyaki, Umaya, Sher-E-Punjab) work fine in a browser. They are only unreadable for this checker.
